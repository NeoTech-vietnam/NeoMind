## Abstract

For the structured research profile, explain the question, method, main finding, and limit in one paragraph. The legacy Cornell profile may omit this section.

## Learning Objectives

Give each research objective an O1-style ID and a checkable outcome. Map every objective to a research question or practice exercise.

## Context and Constraints

State the intended use, assumptions, scope, and limits.

## Cue Column

Write questions that test understanding of the mechanisms and failure cases.

## Main Notes

Label each material claim [observed], [source-reported], [expected], or [inferred], and cite source IDs such as [S1].

### Methods

For structured research, describe the sources, comparison or calculation, assumptions, and evidence limits.

### Explanations

Explain the mechanism step by step, using concrete examples and definitions suited to the reader.

### Results

State what the method established. Keep expected worked results separate from measured outcomes.

### Discussion

Explain implications, alternative explanations, applicability, and remaining uncertainty.

### Research Questions

Give each question a Q1-style ID and its objective IDs. Store its supplied reference answer in the separate answer artifact.

## Demonstration

Show concrete inputs, steps, outputs, and a runnable argument vector or worked example.

### Expected Result

Record the specification-derived result before execution.

### Observed Result

Record actual results with evidence IDs, or state that execution remains pending.

## Failure Cases

Explain practical boundaries and conditions that break an assumption.

## Practice

Link separate exercise and answer artifacts. Give research practice P1-style IDs and objective mappings. Provide an argument-vector command, or an explicit manual procedure for structured research. Preserve the user's solution file; a supplied answer or procedure does not establish learner completion.

## Summary

Explain the mechanism, its applicability, and remaining uncertainty in your own words.

## Sources

Record source ID, title, URI, retrieval timestamp, locator, and applicable file/record hash.
